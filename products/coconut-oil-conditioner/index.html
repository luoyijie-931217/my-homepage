HomeProductsConditionerCoconut Oil Conditioner

Product Information

Coconut Oil Deep Repair Conditioner is an intensive nourishing daily treatment developed by Guangdong Shampoo Factory specifically for dry and damaged hair. Centered around the highly acclaimed Coconut Oil and synergized with Argan Oil, Keratin, and Vitamin E, this product builds a unique four-dimensional “Penetrate-Repair-Hydrate-Shine” system. Its Sulfate-Free gentle formula is designed to provide a care experience of deep moisturizing, powerful hair core repair, significant frizz smoothing, and enhanced healthy shine for all hair types, especially dry, damaged, and color-treated hair. It targets and improves hair concerns caused by dehydration and chemical treatments, restoring hair to a supple, smooth, and vibrant state.

We are not only a reliable manufacturer but also your brand co-creation partner, offering comprehensive, in-depth OEM/ODM customization services from formulation to packaging for this product.
